ISLAMABAD

Islamabad, the capital of Pakistan, was officially inaugurated in 1967, replacing Karachi as the nation’s administrative center. Located in the Islamabad Capital Territory, it has a population of over two million and is known for its high standard of living, safety, and greenery. The city is home to important government institutions, foreign embassies, and landmarks such as the Faisal Mosque, Pakistan Monument, and Lok Virsa Museum.


Its strategic location at the northern edge of the Pothohar Plateau also makes it a gateway to the scenic northern areas of Pakistan.
